Publishing and history

New templates start as drafts. Once the checklist above is satisfied, submit it for review — a different Administrator must then approve and publish it; the same person can't review their own submission. Any further edit sends a pending submission back to draft automatically. Changes to an already-published template apply to new documents as soon as they're saved (no re-approval needed for routine edits).

Generated PDFs remain unchanged. Restore creates a new version; it never erases earlier versions.

Section order, columns, labels, typography, branding, notes and signature wording are customizable. Transaction values come from the recorded movement.

The page below shows the paper boundary, margins, and approximate page-break points for the current page size, orientation, and margin setting — a visual guide, not a pixel-exact match to the final PDF.
